    Where point values come from
    • Transfer partner, airline and hotel values: NerdWallet, October 2026.
    • Cash back and bank travel portal values: each bank's website, November 2026.
    • Capital One does not publish a cash back value, so 1¢ is used.
    Ways to redeem points
    Cash back
    Redeem points as a statement credit or deposit. Simplest, and usually the lowest value.
    Bank travel portal
    Book flights or hotels through the bank's travel site (e.g. Chase Travel), paying with points at a set rate.
    Transfer partners
    Move points to an airline or hotel partner and book flights or nights with them. Usually the most value, but takes a little work. For some banks the value depends on which card you have.
    Loyalty programs
    Loyalty points from a co-branded card (e.g. Delta miles, Marriott points) are spent on bookings with that brand and its partners.
